Pipeline

How It Works

Four clean stages from camera input to glowing output

01
📷

Camera Input

Captures live 720p frames at 30 fps from your built-in or USB webcam. Frames are mirrored for a natural selfie view.

→
02
🧠

AI Detection

MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation separates body pixels from background at 70%+ confidence. Temporal smoothing across 3 frames prevents flicker.

→
03
✏️

Outline Generation

Contour extraction and Canny edge detection create smooth body edges. Glow is layered with multi-pass Gaussian blur.

→
04
🖥️

Live Display

The styled outline is composited onto the live frame with FPS counter, detection badge, and style bar — all in real-time.
